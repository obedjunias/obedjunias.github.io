New preprint alert! We introduce **LOGICAL-COMMONSENSEQA**, a benchmark designed to isolate multi-fact and compositional inference in LLMs using logical operators (AND, OR, NEITHER/NOR). Our evaluation reveals that fluent reasoning often masks underlying logical failures, especially in negation-based tasks. Now accepted at ACL 2026 — check the paper on [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-short.61/)!

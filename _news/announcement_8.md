New preprint alert! We introduce **LOGICAL-COMMONSENSEQA**, a benchmark designed to isolate multi-fact and compositional inference in LLMs using logical operators (AND, OR, NEITHER/NOR). Our evaluation reveals that fluent reasoning often masks underlying logical failures, especially in negation-based tasks. Check the paper on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16504)!

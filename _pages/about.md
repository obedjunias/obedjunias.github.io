I am a researcher at the HUBBS Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder, working with [Dr. Theodora Chaspari](https://www.colorado.edu/cs/theodora-chaspari). I am also advised by [Dr. Maria L. Pacheco](https://blast-cu.github.io/mlpacheco/) in the [BLAST Lab](https://blast-cu.github.io/). My research is driven by a fundamental question: **why do language models behave the way they do?** I am interested in how models acquire capabilities through training, how they represent and use information internally, and why these capabilities sometimes deteriorate. I currently investigate these questions through behavioral evaluation and interpretability.

Before grad school, I worked at Oracle as a Senior Member of Technical Staff. I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at CU Boulder in 2026.

I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

### My recent work includes

- **[LOGICAL-COMMONSENSEQA](/logical-csqa/) ([ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-short.61/))**, a benchmark investigating how language models compose plausibility judgments with logical constraint.
- A follow-up on [structured compositional reasoning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12836), where I look at how to help models handle the logical combinations they struggled with in the benchmark. 
- **Model collapse**, where I study how recursive training on synthetic data changes a model's behavior and its internal representations. 
- Assessing demographic bias in language-based depression detection ([IEEE-BHI 2025](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11269509)).

A lot of this sits close in the intersection of NLP and cognitive science, and I enjoy working across fields. Feel free to [reach out](mailto:obed.junias@colorado.edu) if you'd like to discuss research.
